# Modes: pick the surface first

Pick the mode from the requested surface, not from the product. A tool's landing page is Persuade. A fashion house's docs are Read. (impeccable)

| Mode | The visitor... | Examples | Load next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Persuade** | decides and acts. Design is the product. | Landing page, marketing, campaign, pricing | `taste` run, [bans-and-tells](bans-and-tells.md), `../../craft/reference/mode-persuade.md` |
| **Operate** | completes a task. | App UI, dashboard, editor, admin, settings | [operate](operate.md), `../../taste/references/app-screens.md`, `../../craft/reference/mode-operate.md` |
| **Read** | understands something. | Docs, articles, guides, help, changelog | [operate](operate.md) (Read notes), `../../craft/reference/mode-read.md` |
| **Experience** | is inside the work. | Portfolio, gallery, showcase | `taste` run, `../../craft/reference/new-work.md` |

## Winning rules

1. **Scale ambition to the mode.** Persuade and Experience get full commitment: a point of view, bold work. On Operate and Read, ambition shows as precision and restraint. Known patterns earn their place. (impeccable; UX law 3, Jakob)
2. **Page or app screen?** A page is read and scrolled. An app screen is worked in. A product site that shows its app is a page. The fragments of the app on it follow app-screen rules inside. (taste)
3. **The brief wins over taste defaults.** If the user pins a font, era, material, or palette, honor it. It never overrides accessibility or a line marked as a ban in `craft-floor.md`. (impeccable, C13)
4. **Refinement keeps the look. Redesign replaces it.** Do not split the difference. (impeccable)
5. **Missing `DESIGN.md` does not mean greenfield.** Inspect the visual truth first. Then decide whether to keep, expand, or replace. (impeccable)
