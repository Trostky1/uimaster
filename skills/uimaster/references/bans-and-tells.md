# Bans and tells (anti-"AI slop")

A tell is a device that makes a page read as generated. Most began as good ideas. They became tells by being produced by default, without a reason. (taste tells.md) Reaching for one when the axis is free means you were not deciding. Rewrite the element. Do not soften it.

Removal is not enough. A page with every tell removed and nothing in its place is empty, and that is a tell too. Fill the space with an idea from the subject, with scale, with full content, with a picture made for this subject, or with a flat field of colour. Leave one deliberate rest per page at most.

## Banned on Persuade and Experience pages (no brief earns these back)
- Eyebrow or kicker above a heading. Pill badge above the headline ("New", "Beta"). Section numbers ("01 / 02 / 03", "Step 02") for decoration.
- Gradient text. Violet or indigo gradients. Glowing buttons. Glass or blur panels as decoration. Floating pill nav with blur.
- Yellow-cream ground with terracotta or rust accent.
- Same-size icon-plus-heading-plus-text cards as page structure. Nested cards. Hero-metric template (big number, small label, supporting stats).
- Coloured `border-left` or `border-right` above 1px on cards, list items, callouts.
- Hard offset shadows outside a neobrutalist world. Soft grey shadow under every card.
- Emoji or Unicode glyphs as icons. Icons come from one library or authored SVG, with one stroke and weight.
- Monospace as a "technical" label. Tiny uppercase letter-spaced captions. Agency micro-meta (local time, coordinates, version stamps).
- Decorative hairlines, crosshairs, crop marks, dashed guides.
- Fake dashboards made of grey bars. Gradient blobs. Sketch-style SVG scenes. Stripe or grid backgrounds with no real canvas under them.
- Invented social proof, logos, quotes, or numbers.
- Geometric masks that fake a photo's edge. A modal for a task that needs no interruption.

## Warned (needs a reason you can state in one sentence)
- Bento grid of rounded tiles as the feature section. Everything centred. Stock section order. Every section the same layout. One accent word styled in a headline. Light or dark picked by category.

## Where the rulings change this list
- **App screens** (C1, C11): quiet borders, small labels, and repeated panels are normal. The page bans on cards and lines do not apply. Gradients, glows, tinted icon squares, rainbow charts, and fake numbers stay banned.
- **Real flows** (C3): "Step 2 of 4" progress is required. Only decorative numbering is banned.
- **Real status** (C9): a labelled error, success, live, or beta state is allowed. Decorative badges are not.
- **Destructive actions** (C6): confirmation dialogs are required.
- **Code, data, measurement** (C10): monospace is correct. Tables and form fields may have lines. A real UI fragment marked `data-ui` keeps its own borders.
- **The user's brief** (C13) can earn back any default on this list. It cannot earn back a line marked as a ban in `craft-floor.md`, and it never overrides accessibility.
