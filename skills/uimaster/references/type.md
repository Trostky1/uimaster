# Type

## Winning rules
- **Measure:** body text 65-75 characters per line. Dense data may run wider. (craft-floor, S3 operate)
- **Display size:** 6rem maximum. **Tracking floor:** -0.04em. -0.02 to -0.03em usually reads better. (craft-floor)
- **Headings:** balanced. Headlines run two lines or fewer at every width. A headline that must say what the product is may be longer: add a short sentence under it. Never cut meaning to fit. (taste house rule, C8)
- **Scale:** few sizes, far apart. Five sizes do the work. More than nine near-equal sizes is a tell. Weight and size steps must be obvious. (taste, craft-floor)
- **Operate and Read:** one family is often right. Fixed rem scale, no fluid headings. Step ratio 1.125-1.2. (S3 operate)
- **Persuade and Experience:** display type may carry the voice. Use decisive contrast. (S3 typeset)
- **Families:** two typefaces at most, usually one. Source and self-host a face whose character fits. A system display face (Impact, Arial Black, platform sans) is not a display voice. (taste, craft-floor)
- **Monospace** is for code, data, and measurement. It is never a "technical" costume or a label style. Use tabular figures where numbers align. (taste, craft-floor, C10)
- **No styled accent word** in a headline (italic, serif, colour, underline). The words carry the emphasis. (taste)
- **Test with real copy** at every breakpoint. Fix what overflows. (craft-floor)
- **Chunk text.** Short paragraphs, clear headings, concise labels. (UX law 5, Miller)
