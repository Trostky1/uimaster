# Colour

## Winning rules
- **Contrast:** body and placeholder text 4.5:1 or more. Large text 3:1 or more. On a coloured surface, tint secondary text from that hue or the foreground. Never plain gray. (craft-floor)
- **Never colour alone.** Pair colour with text, icon, or shape for state. (accessibility, UX law 12)
- **Roles and proportion:** one ground, one ink, one accent, plus fields. The accent appears on the single action and one detail, not in ten places. (taste, UX law 7 Von Restorff)
- **Operate:** restrained. Accent colour marks primary action, selection, and state only. Keep a full state vocabulary: hover, focus, active, disabled, selected, loading, error, warning, success, info. (S3 operate)
- **Persuade and Experience:** colour may own large regions and carry the voice when the world calls for it. (S3 colorize)
- **Light or dark** comes from the use scene (who, where, what ambient light), not from the category. (craft-floor)
- **Depth:** shadows carry an offset and a soft blur. A zero-offset coloured halo is decoration. No shadows on things at rest in Persuade pages. Hard offset shadows only in a truly neobrutalist world. (craft-floor, taste)
- **Gradients and glass:** no gradient text. No glass or blur as decoration. Gradients on marketing pages are banned except a neutral scrim under text on a photo. (craft-floor, taste)
- **Banned palettes:** violet or indigo gradients; yellow-cream ground with terracotta or rust. Take colour from the subject's world. (taste tells)
- **Side-stripe borders:** no coloured `border-left` or `border-right` above 1px on cards, list items, callouts. (craft-floor)
- **Brand first:** keep confirmed brand colours. Do not replace a visual world while "adding colour". (S3 colorize)
