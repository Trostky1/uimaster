# Responsive and native

## Winning rules
- **Check at 360 px and 1440 px** at least. Run the real copy at every breakpoint. (craft-floor, taste)
- **The phone is a second composition,** not a squeezed desktop page. It has its own crop and its own line breaks. The first screen is still one clear thing. (taste layout)
- **Adapt, do not scale.** Rethink the experience for the new context: input method (touch, mouse, keyboard, voice), screen size, connection, and use. (S3 adapt)
- **Touch:** 44x44 px targets. Put frequent actions within thumb reach. Place actions beside the content they affect. (UX laws 2, 8)
- **Native iOS and Android:** do not apply web tooling. Follow `../../craft/reference/ios.md` or `../../craft/reference/android.md`, including platform scaling, insets, navigation, and Reduce Motion. Use `../../craft/reference/adapt.native.md` and `audit.native.md`. (S3; distilled from ehmo/platform-design-skills, MIT)
- **Navigation, search, settings, and account** go where users expect them on that platform. (UX law 3)
- **Performance on weak devices and slow networks** is part of responsive design. (S3 optimize)
